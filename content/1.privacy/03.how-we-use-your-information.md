ResumAI uses the information we collect to deliver our services, improve the platform, and provide a secure experience for all users.

We process your information only for legitimate business purposes and in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

## How We Use Your Information

Your information may be used to:

- Provide AI-powered resume analysis and candidate matching.
- Create and manage hiring projects.
- Generate candidate reports and recommendations.
- Improve the accuracy and quality of our AI services.
- Authenticate users and maintain account security.
- Respond to support requests and user feedback.
- Detect fraud, abuse, or unauthorized activity.
- Comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not use your personal information for unrelated purposes without your consent.
