Depending on your location, you may have certain privacy rights regarding your personal information.

We are committed to helping users exercise these rights whenever applicable.

## Your Rights

You may have the right to:

- Access your personal information.
- Correct inaccurate information.
- Delete your account and personal data.
- Export your information.
- Withdraw consent where applicable.
- Contact us regarding privacy-related concerns.

We will respond to valid privacy requests within the time required by applicable law.
