To provide AI-powered resume analysis and candidate management, ResumAI collects certain types of information when you use our platform.

We collect only the information necessary to operate our services, improve user experience, and ensure the security of your account.

## Information You Provide

Depending on how you use ResumAI, you may provide us with:

- **Account Information**, such as your name, email address, and authentication details.
- **Resumes and CVs**, including work experience, education, skills, certifications, and other information contained in uploaded documents.
- **Job Descriptions**, requirements, responsibilities, and hiring criteria created within your projects.
- **Project Information**, including project names, candidate lists, notes, and other hiring-related content.
- **Support Requests**, feedback, or other communications you send to our team.

## Information Collected Automatically

When you access ResumAI, we may automatically collect certain technical information, including:

- Device and browser information.
- IP address.
- Operating system.
- Pages visited and features used.
- Date and time of your requests.
- Application logs required for security and troubleshooting.

## AI Processing Data

When you upload resumes or job descriptions, their content may be processed by our AI systems to:

- Extract skills, experience, education, and qualifications.
- Compare candidates against job requirements.
- Generate resume analyses and compatibility scores.
- Provide recommendations and hiring insights.

This processing is performed solely to deliver the features of ResumAI.

## What We Do Not Collect

We do not intentionally collect information beyond what is required to operate our platform.

We do not sell your personal information or use uploaded resumes to build public datasets or advertise to third parties.
