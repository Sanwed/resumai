Welcome to the ResumAI Privacy Policy.

At ResumAI, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, how we protect it, and the choices you have regarding your information when using our platform.

ResumAI is an AI-powered platform that helps recruiters evaluate candidates and helps job seekers improve their resumes through intelligent resume analysis and matching.

By using ResumAI, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

## What This Policy Covers

This Privacy Policy explains:

- **What information we collect**, including account details, resumes, and job descriptions.
- **How we use your information** to provide AI-powered resume analysis and improve our services.
- **How we protect your data** through secure storage and industry-standard security practices.
- **When information may be shared** with trusted service providers required to operate the platform.
- **Your privacy rights**, including accessing, updating, exporting, or deleting your personal information.

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or how your information is processed, please contact us using the information provided in the **Contact** section.
