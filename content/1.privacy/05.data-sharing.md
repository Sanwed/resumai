ResumAI does not sell your personal information.

We only share information when necessary to operate our services or comply with legal obligations.

## Who We May Share Data With

Your information may be shared with:

- Trusted AI service providers responsible for resume analysis.
- Authentication providers used to secure your account.
- Cloud hosting and infrastructure providers.
- Database and storage providers.
- Legal authorities when required by applicable law.

All third-party providers are required to protect your information and process it only for authorized purposes.
