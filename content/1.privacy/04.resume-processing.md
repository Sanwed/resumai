ResumAI uses artificial intelligence to analyze uploaded resumes and compare them with job descriptions provided by users.

The purpose of this processing is to generate meaningful insights that assist recruiters and job seekers.

## How AI Processing Works

Our AI systems may:

- Extract skills, education, certifications, and work experience.
- Identify relevant qualifications.
- Compare resumes with job requirements.
- Generate compatibility scores.
- Highlight strengths and potential gaps.
- Produce AI-generated summaries and recommendations.

AI-generated results are intended to assist decision-making and should not be considered the sole basis for hiring or employment decisions.
