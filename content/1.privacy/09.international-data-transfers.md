ResumAI may use cloud services and infrastructure located in different countries to operate our platform efficiently.

As a result, your information may be processed or stored outside your country of residence.

## International Transfers

When transferring personal information internationally, we take reasonable measures to protect your data, including:

- Working with trusted service providers.
- Using secure data transmission methods.
- Applying contractual safeguards where required.
- Following applicable data protection laws whenever possible.
