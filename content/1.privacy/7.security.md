Protecting your information is one of our highest priorities.

We implement technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

## Security Measures

Our security practices include:

- Secure HTTPS connections.
- Encrypted data transmission.
- Access controls and authentication.
- Regular security monitoring.
- Secure cloud infrastructure.
- Limited employee access to personal information.

Although we strive to protect your information, no system can guarantee absolute security.
