---
title: Data Retention
description: Learn how long ResumAI stores your information.
---

We retain your information only for as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with applicable legal obligations.

## Data Retention Period

We may retain:

- Account information while your account remains active.
- Projects and uploaded resumes until you delete them.
- Backup copies for a limited period to support disaster recovery.
- Technical logs for security and troubleshooting purposes.

When you delete your account, we will remove or anonymize your personal information within a reasonable period unless we are legally required to retain it.
