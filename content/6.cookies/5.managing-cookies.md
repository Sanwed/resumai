Most web browsers allow you to control or delete cookies through their settings.

Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality and performance of ResumAI.

## Your Choices

You can:

- **Delete existing cookies** from your browser.
- **Block future cookies** through browser settings.
- **Configure cookie preferences** where available.
- **Continue using the platform** with essential cookies enabled.

For more information, consult your browser's documentation.
