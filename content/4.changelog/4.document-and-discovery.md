This release expands document support and improves how ResumAI's public pages are understood and shared.

**Document improvements:**

- 📝 Upload and analyze DOCX resumes in addition to PDF files
- 🛠️ Receive clearer feedback when a document cannot be parsed or analyzed completely

**Public-page improvements:**

- 🔎 More descriptive page titles and search descriptions
- 🖼️ Branded social preview images for public pages
- 🧭 Structured page, product, article, and breadcrumb information for search engines
- ♿ Improved alternative text for avatars, images, and social previews
- 🐦 Consistent ResumAI attribution in Twitter and X cards

Together, these changes make the product easier to use with common resume formats and easier to understand before visiting it.
