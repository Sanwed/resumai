🚀 Today marks an exciting milestone for ResumAI — our platform is officially available in **Public Beta**.

ResumAI is an AI-powered recruitment platform designed to help recruiters evaluate candidates faster and help job seekers improve their resumes with intelligent analysis.

Our mission is simple: reduce the time spent reviewing resumes while providing clear, data-driven insights that support better hiring decisions.

**What's included in this release:**

- 🤖 AI-powered resume analysis and scoring
- 📄 Resume upload with instant processing
- 🎯 Job description matching
- 📊 Candidate strengths and improvement suggestions
- 🔐 Secure authentication and account management
- 🎨 Modern interface built with Nuxt UI
- 🌙 Light and Dark mode support
- 📱 Responsive design for desktop and mobile devices

**Built for recruiters and job seekers**

Recruiters can quickly compare resumes against job requirements, while candidates receive actionable feedback to improve their resumes before applying for new opportunities.

Our AI focuses on identifying relevant skills, experience, and potential gaps to simplify the hiring process.

**What's next?** ✨

This Public Beta is only the beginning. Upcoming releases will introduce:

- 📈 Advanced candidate comparison
- 🧠 Improved AI recommendations
- 📂 Project and workspace management
- 👥 Team collaboration features
- 📊 Analytics dashboard
- ⚡ Faster document processing
- 🌍 Additional language support

We're excited to continue improving ResumAI based on your feedback. Thank you for joining us at the beginning of this journey! 💜
