ResumAI is officially available in **Public Beta**.

This first public release introduces a focused way for recruiters and job seekers to understand how well a resume matches a role. Instead of returning a single opaque score, ResumAI turns each review into structured, actionable feedback.

**Included in the public beta:**

- 🤖 AI-assisted resume analysis grounded in the supplied job description
- 🎯 Candidate compatibility scoring with a clear hiring verdict
- 💡 Strengths, skill gaps, red flags, and suggested interview questions
- 📄 Secure resume upload and document processing
- 🔐 Account creation, email verification, sign-in, and password recovery
- 🎨 A responsive interface with light and dark themes

**Built for clearer decisions**

Recruiters can identify promising candidates and prepare more relevant interviews. Job seekers can see which parts of their resume support a role and where important evidence may be missing.

Public Beta gives us room to keep improving analysis quality, document handling, and the overall workflow while the core product remains available for real-world use.
